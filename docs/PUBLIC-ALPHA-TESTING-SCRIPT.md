# Pathoro — Public Alpha Testing Script

**Production URL:** https://pathoro-mvp.vercel.app

This is the script for running Pathoro's first small public alpha. Share the link, let the tester click through on their own (think-aloud style if they're comfortable), and take notes on where they hesitate, get confused, or light up.

## What Pathoro is trying to test

Pathoro is not a search engine and not a content feed. It's trying to be a **map to opportunity**: given a goal, it lays out the path, compares the real branches within that path, scouts real-world access points along the way, and connects the person to trail markers and people who've walked it before.

This alpha is testing whether that idea actually lands for a real person, not just in our own heads:

- Does the path/map framing read as more useful than a generic search result?
- Do the branch comparisons (AI resilience, demand, income, cost, autonomy) help someone actually decide something?
- Does "trail notes" feel like real, attached wisdom rather than a comment section?
- Does "Path Guide" feel like a genuinely useful next step, or like a gimmick?
- What's still missing, confusing, or fake-feeling at this stage?

## Suggested test journeys

Pick one (or let the tester pick a path close to something real in their life). All three are fully wired end to end.

### 1. Licensed Therapist
1. Land on the homepage, click **Begin Your Journey**
2. Enter a goal like "become a licensed therapist" and a city
3. Continue to the Orientation Map (Compass Mode)
4. Open the **detailed Trail Map**
5. Compare a couple of branches (e.g. Clinical Mental Health Counselor vs. Marriage & Family Therapist)
6. Read a trail note or two
7. Click **Find a guide** on the Path Guide card and submit a question

### 2. Registered Nurse
1. From the homepage or `/trail-map?goal=nurse`, open the Trail Map directly
2. Compare RN, ICU/Critical Care, Nurse Practitioner, and Travel Nursing
3. Check the AI replacement risk / AI resilience reading — does it feel right that nursing shows as low risk?
4. Look at the Path Guide card

### 3. Resale Business
1. From the homepage or `/trail-map?goal=resale`, open the Trail Map directly
2. Compare a couple of sourcing paths (e.g. Flea Market Sourcing vs. eBay Resale)
3. Read the trail notes — do they feel like real advice from someone who's done it?
4. Look at the Path Guide card and its "Verified experience" badge

## Questions to ask testers

Ask these after they've clicked through, before offering any explanation of your own:

1. Did this feel like Google results, or did it feel like a map to opportunity?
2. Would you rather leave advice here than on Reddit/LinkedIn because it's tied to a real path?
3. Did the Trail Map help you understand your options?
4. Did the Path Guide idea feel valuable?
5. What felt fake, confusing, or missing?
